ABOUT THE CLUB

A student newsroom for campus life.

The Media Club is NMA College's student-run media team. We document events, tell student and graduate stories, and keep the college's social channels alive with real, student-made content.

Our vision

To make NMA College the most visible, best-documented student community among Myanmar's private colleges.

Our mission

To capture NMA student life in photo, video, and words made by students themselves, and to give members real media skills they can use after graduation.

Why a media club

  • It helps admissions. A 30-second TikTok from a real student beats a printed brochure. Student-made content feels honest, and honesty earns trust.
  • It records events properly. Graduations, career fairs, and guest talks disappear after they happen. We turn each one into photos, recaps, and interviews that keep working for weeks.
  • It builds careers. Members graduate with a real portfolio: shooting, editing, design, writing, and social media management.
  • It brings students together. Members from every program and year work on one project with one shared identity.

HOW WE RUN

Student-led, advisor-guided.

A faculty advisor sets policy and approves sensitive posts. Students run everything else.

President
Sets direction, liaises with the School Admission Team, final sign-off.
Vice President
Day-to-day operations, scheduling, stands in for the president.
Content Lead
Runs the editorial calendar, assigns stories, checks quality.
Video Lead
Plans shoots, sets editing standards, looks after gear.
Design Lead
Graphics, thumbnails, templates, brand compliance.
Social Media Manager
Publishes, writes captions, replies to comments, tracks stats.
Secretary / Treasurer
Member records, budget, meeting notes.

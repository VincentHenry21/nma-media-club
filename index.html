ABOUT THE CLUB

A student newsroom for campus life.

The Media Club is NMA College's student-run media team, founded in 2026. We document events, tell student and graduate stories, and keep the college's social channels alive with real, student-made content.

Our vision

To make NMA College the most visible, best-documented student community among Myanmar's private colleges.

Our mission

To capture NMA student life in photo, video, and words made by students themselves, and to give members real media skills they can use after graduation.

    HOW WE RUN

    Student-led, advisor-guided.

    A faculty advisor sets content policy and approves sensitive posts. Students run everything else. The executive team is elected every academic year.

    Members

    Any NMA student can join, any program, any year. New members complete a two-week onboarding in phone shooting, CapCut editing, and the club brand guide, then pick a team: video, design, or writing and social. Target size for year one: 15 to 25 active members.
